Privacy Policy

How GESMA handles the information you share with us

Overview

GESMA (Geneva English-Speaking Muslims Association) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We only collect the information we genuinely need to respond to you and run our community programmes. We handle personal data in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible for your data

The body responsible for the personal data collected through this website (the "data controller") is:

Geneva English-Speaking Muslims Association (GESMA)
Geneva, Switzerland
Email: info@gesma.ch

What we collect

When you contact us through the form on our website, you may choose to share:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • The programme you are interested in and any message you write to us

We do not collect any of this information unless you choose to send it to us.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and answer your questions.
  • To discuss the most suitable programme for your child or family.
  • To keep you informed about enrolment, sessions, and schedules you have asked about.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

How your message reaches us (transfer abroad)

Our contact form is delivered to us by a third-party form service, Web3Forms, which forwards your message to our email inbox. This means the details you submit are processed on servers located in the United States of America. The USA is recognised by Switzerland as providing adequate data protection under the Swiss–US Data Privacy Framework. Your details are used only to deliver and respond to your enquiry.

Cookies and tracking

Our website does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not use analytics that profile you. Our embedded Google Map does not load automatically — it only loads, and may set Google cookies, if you choose to click "Load Google Map". Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details.

How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry messages only for as long as needed to respond to you and support your involvement with GESMA — typically no longer than the duration of our correspondence or your family's participation in our programmes, after which we delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under Swiss data protection law (and the GDPR where it applies) you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict its processing.

To exercise any of these rights, just email info@gesma.ch and we will help.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) — www.edoeb.admin.ch. If you are in the EU, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

Where enquiries concern a child, we ask that they be made by a parent or guardian. We process a child's personal data only with the involvement and consent of their parent or guardian.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at info@gesma.ch.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Last updated June 2026.
