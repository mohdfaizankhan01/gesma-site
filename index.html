Who We Are

A Home for Muslims
in the Heart of Geneva

Geneva is one of the most international cities in the world — yet for many English-speaking Muslim families, finding a community that truly feels like home can be a real challenge. GESMA was built to change that.

We are a community association dedicated to nurturing the faith, identity, and confidence of English-speaking Muslims across Geneva and its surroundings. From structured Islamic education for children to open discussions for teenagers and parents — everything we do is designed to help families thrive, not just survive, as Muslims in the West.

We believe that being a good Muslim and a proud member of Swiss society are not in conflict — they go hand in hand. Our community is welcoming, multicultural, and built on values of respect, service, and genuine belonging.

Join Our Community

For any English-speaking Muslim family in or around Geneva. Whether you arrived last month or have lived here for years, GESMA is your community. Our programs serve children from age 6, teenagers, and parents — all taught in English, in a welcoming multicultural setting.

Most Islamic education in Geneva is in French or Arabic — leaving English-speaking families with very few options. GESMA fills that gap with a structured, English-medium curriculum trusted by scholars across the UK and South Asia, in a warm environment that genuinely understands the international Muslim experience.

Not at all. You're welcome to reach out, visit, and enrol your children without any formal membership. We believe the community should be open and accessible to everyone — just get in touch and we'll guide you through the next steps.

Classes run every Friday from 5:30 to 7:30 PM. Our venue details are coming soon — contact us directly and we'll share everything you need to find us and get started.

About Our Curriculum:

Our syllabus is built on the An-Nasihah Islamic Curriculum — a comprehensive, structured programme developed by leading Shaikhs and Ulama from Darul Uloom Leicester, Dewsbury, Bury, and Fatah al-Dawah (India & South Africa). Refined over many years, it is widely regarded as one of the most trusted English-medium Islamic education frameworks available today. Explore the curriculum →
