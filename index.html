Why GESMA Exists

Geneva is one of the world's most international cities, home to families from many cultures, languages, and backgrounds. Yet for many English-speaking Muslim families, it can still be difficult to find Islamic learning and community activities that feel accessible, relevant, and welcoming. GESMA was created to help fill this gap.

We support Muslim children, youth, and families in Geneva and surrounding areas through structured Islamic education, guided discussions, family connection, and community activities — all in English.

Our aim is to help young Muslims strengthen their relationship with Allah, grow in Islamic knowledge and character, feel confident in their Muslim identity, and contribute positively to the multicultural society around them.

Join Our Community

GESMA welcomes English-speaking Muslim children, teenagers, young adults, and families from Geneva and surrounding areas. Our community includes both international and locally based families from a wide range of cultural and professional backgrounds.

No. Our programmes are designed to accommodate children with different levels of knowledge and experience. We aim to support each child's learning journey in a positive and encouraging environment.

Yes. All classes, discussions, and learning activities are conducted in English, making them accessible to international and multicultural families.

Children aged 6–9 attend mixed classes. From ages 10–14, boys and girls learn in separate groups to provide an age-appropriate learning environment during the teenage years. For the Youth Discussion Series (ages 15+), brothers and sisters participate in the same session while remaining seated in separate areas, using an interactive, discussion-based format.

Our Islamic Studies programme follows the internationally recognised An-Nasihah Islamic Curriculum, combining Islamic beliefs, worship, Prophetic teachings, Seerah, Islamic history, character development, and daily conduct. The Youth Discussion Series draws on resources from Yaqeen Institute, Al-Nasihah's Living Islam series, and other carefully selected materials.

The Islamic Studies programme is held every Friday evening, for approximately two hours. As the start time can vary through the year, please contact us for the latest timing. The Youth Discussion Series is typically held twice a month. Please contact us for the latest schedule, venue details, and programme availability.

Simply get in touch through the contact form, WhatsApp, or email. We would be happy to answer your questions, discuss the most suitable programme for your child or family, and guide you through the enrolment process.

English-Speaking Environment

Structured Islamic Learning

Ages 6–14 follow the recognised An-Nasihah Curriculum, supporting faith, worship, manners, character, and daily practice.

Guided Youth Discussions

Family-Centred

Welcoming & Diverse
